What is a Solar Storage Battery?

A solar storage battery stores excess electricity generated by your solar panels during the day. This stored energy can be used at night, during cloudy weather, or during power outages, ensuring uninterrupted power supply and better energy management.

Benefits of Solar Battery Storage

Power Backup

Get uninterrupted power supply during grid failures or outages.

Energy Independence

Reduce reliance on electricity providers and rising tariffs.

Maximum Solar Usage

Store excess energy instead of sending it back to the grid.

Cost Savings

Use stored energy during peak hours to reduce electricity bills.
