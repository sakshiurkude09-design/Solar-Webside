What is Residential Solar?

Residential solar, also called rooftop solar, refers to solar panels installed on your home roof that convert sunlight into electricity. These systems can reduce or even replace your electricity bill, provide energy independence, and help the environment.

Benefits of Residential Solar

Lower Electricity Bills

Generate your own electricity and reduce dependency on the grid.

Environmentally Friendly

Solar energy is clean and renewable, helping reduce carbon emissions.

Energy Independence

Produce your own power and protect yourself from rising electricity costs.

Government Subsidies

Many governments provide subsidies to make residential solar more affordable.
